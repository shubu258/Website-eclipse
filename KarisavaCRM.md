# Karisava Hospital CRM — Custom Patient & Operations Management Platform

Karisava required a centralized system to manage patient information, sales operations, documents, receipts, and patient status from a single platform. Their operations involved multiple people handling different parts of the patient journey, making it important to have clear access controls and a reliable source of information for every patient.

We developed a custom CRM tailored specifically to Karisava's workflow. The platform brings patient management, sales activity, documents, receipts, history, and status tracking into one system, allowing the team to manage the complete patient lifecycle without relying on scattered records or manual processes.

## The Challenge

Managing patient information across different records and operational processes can quickly become difficult as the number of patients increases. Information such as patient details, documents, payment receipts, previous interactions, and current status needs to remain accessible while ensuring that each team member only has access to the functionality relevant to their role.

Karisava needed a system that could provide a complete view of every patient while supporting different levels of access for administrators and sales personnel.

The main challenges included:

### Scattered Patient Information

Patient information, documents, history, and operational details needed to be organized in one centralized system instead of being managed across multiple sources.

### Patient Status Management

The team needed to know the current status of every patient and quickly understand where each patient was within the overall process.

### Role-Based Access

Administrators and sales personnel have different responsibilities, requiring separate access levels and permissions within the CRM.

### Document & Receipt Management

Patient-related documents and receipts needed to be stored and associated with the correct patient for easy retrieval and reference.

### Complete Patient History

The team needed visibility into previous patient interactions and records instead of treating every interaction as a new case.

## Our Solution

We built a custom CRM specifically around Karisava's operational workflow.

The platform acts as a centralized patient management system where every patient has a dedicated profile containing their core information, current status, documents, receipts, and historical records.

Rather than building a generic CRM with unnecessary functionality, the system was designed around the actual workflow of the Karisava team.

The platform provides separate experiences for administrators and sales personnel, allowing each role to work with the information and actions relevant to them.

## Centralized Patient Management

Every patient is represented through a dedicated profile that acts as the single source of truth for their information.

The profile can contain patient details, current status, associated documents, receipts, previous records, and other operational information required by the team.

This gives staff a complete picture of the patient without having to search through multiple systems.

A typical patient record can move through the workflow while retaining its complete history, allowing the team to understand both the patient's current state and what happened previously.

## Patient Lifecycle & Status Tracking

A key part of the CRM is the ability to track the current status of each patient.

Instead of simply storing patient information, the system provides an operational view of where patients currently stand.

The team can identify active patients, review their current stage, and access the associated records directly from the CRM.

This makes the platform useful not only as a database but also as a day-to-day operational tool for managing the patient pipeline.

## Admin Dashboard

Administrators have broader access to the system and can manage the overall operation from a centralized dashboard.

The admin interface provides visibility into patient records, sales activity, documents, receipts, and other CRM data.

Administrators can manage users and control access to the platform while maintaining an overview of the organization's patient operations.

This creates a centralized management layer without requiring every user to have unrestricted access to the entire system.

## Sales Person Access

Sales personnel receive a dedicated view focused on the patients and activities relevant to their work.

They can manage assigned patient information, update relevant statuses, review patient history, and maintain the information required during the patient journey.

Role-based access ensures that sales personnel can efficiently perform their responsibilities while sensitive administrative functionality remains restricted.

This separation also makes the interface simpler for each type of user because they only interact with the parts of the system relevant to their role.

## Patient Documents

The CRM provides centralized document management for patient records.

Documents can be associated directly with individual patient profiles, making them easier to locate and manage.

Instead of keeping patient documents separately from the patient's operational record, the CRM connects the documents with the relevant patient and their history.

This creates a more organized record structure and reduces the time required to find supporting documents.

## Receipt Management

Receipts are maintained as part of the patient's overall record.

The system allows the team to associate receipts with the appropriate patient so that payment-related information remains connected to the corresponding record.

This provides staff with a consolidated view of the patient rather than requiring them to search separately for payment records.

## Patient History

Each patient profile maintains a historical record of relevant activity.

This allows authorized users to understand how the patient's record has developed over time, including previous updates, status changes, documents, and other associated information.

Having historical context available within the same profile helps the team continue the patient journey without losing information from previous interactions.

## Role-Based Access Control

Security and access management were built into the CRM around the organization's workflow.

The system differentiates between administrative users and sales personnel, providing each role with appropriate permissions.

This allows Karisava to maintain centralized control over sensitive information while still giving operational teams the access they need to perform their work.

The approach also makes the system scalable as additional users and roles are introduced.

## Technology & System Architecture

The CRM was developed as a custom web-based business application, with the architecture designed around centralized data management, role-based access, and secure handling of patient records.

The system separates the presentation layer, business logic, authentication, authorization, and data management so that the platform can evolve as Karisava's operational requirements grow.

The architecture also allows additional modules and workflows to be introduced without redesigning the entire CRM.

## Business Impact

The CRM transformed Karisava's patient management workflow from fragmented record handling into a centralized digital system.

The team can now access patient information, status, documents, receipts, and historical records from a single platform. Administrators maintain overall visibility and control, while sales personnel have a dedicated workflow for managing their assigned operational responsibilities.

Most importantly, the system was built around Karisava's actual workflow rather than forcing the organization to adapt its processes to a generic CRM.

## One Platform for the Complete Patient Journey

The Karisava CRM brings the operational side of patient management into one connected system.

From the first patient record to ongoing status updates, documents, receipts, and historical information, every important piece of information remains connected to the patient's profile.

The result is a custom CRM that gives Karisava a structured, role-based, and scalable foundation for managing its patient operations while providing the team with a clearer view of every active patient and their complete journey.
